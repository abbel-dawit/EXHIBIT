Four steps to living art

  1. ✓
    01

    Scan your private QR

    Find the QR code that came with your poster. Scan it with your phone camera or tap Unlock here. Each code is unique to your copy.

  2. 02

    Point at the poster

    Open it from My Posters, tap Start AR and fit the whole poster in the frame. Stand back a little at first.

  3. 03

    Watch it come alive

    The light moves, the sky drifts, embers rise. Move your phone and the artwork moves with you, always fully on screen.

  4. ✦
    04

    Keep it forever

    Your edition is signed by the artist and saved on your phone, so it works offline. Back it up anywhere with one tap.

Tips for a perfect lock

  • Stand back until the whole poster fits on screen.
  • Avoid glare: tilt slightly if lights reflect off the print.
  • Even light helps. Very dark rooms make it harder.
  • Fit mode keeps the artwork fully on screen. Pin mode locks it onto the print. Switch with the frame button.
